Key Takeaways  

[1] Overview

- 90-minute, two-question Scaled Assessment  
- first round of interviews (one technical, one behavioral)  
- second round of two technical interviews (in person)  
<br>

[2] Rounds
- Round 1:  
    - technical skills (Data Structures, Algorithms)  
    - behavioral competencies
- Round 2:  
    - entirely technical  
    - potentailly including a domain-specific interview  
<br>
