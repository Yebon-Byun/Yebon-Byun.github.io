### Introduction
Developed a real-time sensitive data de-identification system for an AI contact center, responsible for detecting and masking personal information in live user utterances. The system processes over 1,000 production queries per day, where accuracy, latency, and reliability are critical due to privacy and compliance requirements.

### Skills
- Python
- Machine Learning
- Natural Language Processing
- PyTorch
- Production ML Systems

### Implementation
- Delivered 98% F1-score in sensitive data de-identification by serving 1K+ daily privacy queries in an AI Contact Center through engineering and deploying a BiLSTM-CRF model in PyTorch with 500K+ utterances, and hyperparameter tuning.  
- Enhanced BLEU score from 24% (27.5 → 34.2) in a bilingual English–Korean AI note-taking service by fine-tuning Meta’s 418M-parameter LLM (M2M-100) model with HuggingFace Transformers and RAG-based context modeling.  

### My Role
- Owned the end-to-end development and deployment of the de-identification model and inference pipeline.  
- Improved model performance to achieve a 98% F1-score on sensitive entity detection.  
- Addressed failure modes such as partial masking, malformed inputs, and edge-case entity boundaries.  
- Collaborated with product and platform teams to ensure safe deployment and operational stability.  

### Insights
-Learned the importance of treating ML models as part of a larger system rather than isolated components.  
- Balancing accuracy with latency and failure tolerance was essential in a high-trust, production environment.  
- Designing for observability and graceful degradation significantly improved system reliability.
